## Proposed Post

Most businesses automate the wrong things first. 🚀

I've watched dozens of companies jump into AI automation — and almost all of them start with the same mistake: they automate tasks they *can* automate, not the ones that actually move the needle.

Here's what I see repeatedly:
1. A team spends 3 months automating email sorting
2. It saves 20 minutes per day
3. Meanwhile, their manual lead qualification is leaking $50K/month in missed opportunities

The real ROI in business automation isn't in convenience — it's in compounding leverage.

The highest-value automations I've built share one thing: they eliminate bottlenecks in revenue-generating workflows, not admin tasks.

Before automating anything, ask yourself:
→ Where does work pile up and create delays?
→ Where do humans make the same decision repeatedly?
→ What would happen if this process ran 24/7 without anyone watching?

That's where AI belongs. 🤖

One rule I follow: automate decisions before you automate tasks. Decisions scale infinitely. Task savings plateau.

What's one automation in your business that actually moved the needle? 💡
