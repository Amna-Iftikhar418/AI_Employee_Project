## Post Content

90% of automation projects fail within the first year.

Not because of bad technology. Not because of budget. Because of this one mistake most teams make on day one:

They automate a broken process.

Here's what I mean:

A broken process running manually = 1 broken process.
A broken process running automatically = a broken process at scale, 24/7, generating errors nobody notices until it's too late.

I've seen this destroy real businesses:
→ An e-commerce brand automated order confirmations — but the template had the wrong return policy. 10,000 wrong emails sent in 48 hours.
→ A SaaS company automated lead scoring — on bad data. Their sales team chased dead leads for 3 months.

The rule nobody teaches: before you automate, standardize. Before you standardize, simplify.

Automation amplifies what's already there — good or broken. 🔥

If your process isn't clean today, making it faster just creates faster problems.
