## Post Content

Consistency in coding is not about motivation — it's about systems.

Last year, I missed 3 weeks of coding in a row. Not because I was lazy. Because I was waiting to feel "ready."

That was the wrong approach.

Here's what actually works for me now:

1. Code for 25 minutes before checking anything else. Not an hour. Not "when I have time." Just 25 minutes — first thing. ⏱

2. Keep a friction log. Every time I struggle to start, I write down the obstacle. After a week, patterns emerge. I fix the patterns, not the symptoms.

3. Ship something every Friday. Even a tiny utility. Even a CLI flag. Shipping creates momentum that motivation can't.

4. Never miss twice. One missed day is an accident. Two is a new habit — the wrong one. 🔁

The secret is not discipline. It's designing your environment so coding is the easiest option. Remove every excuse before it forms.

What's the one system or habit that keeps your coding consistent when motivation disappears?
